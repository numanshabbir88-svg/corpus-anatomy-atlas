CORPUS ANATOMY ATLAS

NETLIFY DEPLOY: Start with NETLIFY-GIT-DEPLOY.md. Extract the final ZIP and put its contents at the ROOT of your GitHub repository; do not upload the source ZIP to Netlify Drop. Netlify runs the build and publishes site/ automatically.

Open index.html in a modern browser after extracting this folder. All CSS, JavaScript and images are stored locally in the folder; no account or external library is required. For a local compressed preview, run `python3 server.py --port 3000` inside the corpus folder and open http://localhost:3000 in your own browser.

Contents: 74 anatomy chapters with at least 20 substantive anatomical or developmental entries per chapter (1,586 chapter entries total), 207 quiz questions, a 206-bone atlas, 45 joint families and 312 muscle records. The male and female section hubs are #/male and #/female; their reproductive-system overview chapters are #/maleoverview and #/femaleoverview.

In chapter pages, thin label lines on realistic images mark only structures represented on that regional image. Gold-bordered “Study note” entries add deeper anatomy without claiming an exact pin or histology photo. Search works for both pinned structures and study notes.

Educational reference only. Digital renders are not patient photographs or diagnostic images. See chapter source links and the site footer for reference context.

Bandwidth: the site displays compact WebP plates and smaller thumbnails, loads most images only when needed, and caches assets in the optional local server. The original JPG/PNG plates are also retained in img/ for reference. Full anatomy text, chapters and quizzes are still available offline.

Search-readable study pages are in learn/. Every one of the 74 chapter pages has five visible, chapter-specific questions and answers (370 total), breadcrumbs, and 3–4 relevant related-guide links. All chapters receive at least one related-guide link from another article. Edit title, intro/meta description, Q&A and editorial update date in seo/chapters/<chapter>.json; see seo/README.md. Git-connected Netlify rebuilds and publishes automatically on commit. A drag-and-drop site needs another manual upload after every content edit. Static indexed HTML cannot change on the live site without a new publication. The reading edition includes an illustrated featured-guide layout, system shortcuts, light accessible motion and an article reading indicator. For a real deployment, run python3 build_seo.py && python3 verify_seo.py with a valid HTTPS SITE_URL (or use the Netlify build configuration). See SEO-DEPLOY-CHECKLIST.md. The generated site/ directory is the lean production publish folder. Canonicals and the 78-URL sitemap are deliberately not given a fake address in the noindex local preview. A free Netlify site address is sufficient for publishing; buying a custom domain is optional.

New foundation batch: #/anatomicallanguage, #/bodyregions, #/tissuefoundations. Digital tissue cutaways are NOT diagnostic histology slides. See COVERAGE-ROADMAP.md for existing, partial and still-pending user-requested topics; no claim of complete medical-school coverage.

New conception-to-birth study path: 11 separate chapters at #/spermanatomy, #/oocyteanatomy, #/fertilization, #/cleavageblastocyst, #/implantation, #/placentaanatomy, #/embryodevelopment, #/fetaldevelopment, #/laborfirststage, #/laborsecondstage and #/laborthirdstage. Each has 20 substantive entries, three quiz questions, five editable visible SEO Q&As and an 11-step navigational pathway. The expanded reproduction picture library has 22 original digital plates (17 AI-assisted educational drawings plus five original cell-stage schematics), plus existing contextual anatomy plates. Dedicated digital illustrations now show illustrative middle and later fetal growth and distinct first, second and third labor stages. The 220 entries each open a relevant enlarged plate and include its honest context note; five microscopic-scale tissue plates also open via an organ-to-cell comparison. These are not clinical photos, histology slides or scans; a particular named microscopic cell, body movement, exact cervical dilation or complete birth is NOT necessarily separately visible in its picture. Never use the atlas for patient assessment or childbirth instructions.
