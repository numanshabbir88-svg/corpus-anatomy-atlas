About this project

An atlas built for learning.

Corpus Anatomy Atlas pairs original interactive diagrams with educational digital anatomy illustrations and written descriptions of visible and deeper structures. The reading edition gives each chapter a stable, accessible page. The interactive edition adds numbered pins, animations, search, body maps and quizzes.

Our editorial approach

Entries organize anatomical location, function and clinical relevance using standard anatomy references and further-reading links. Digital illustrations orient the learner; they are not patient photographs, histology specimens or proof that every microscopic structure is visible on a single plate. Some content and illustrations were created or edited with software-assisted methods. We do not invent medical qualifications, patient cases, endorsements or research findings.

Medical and surgical guidance changes. These pages are educational summaries, not diagnosis, treatment advice or a substitute for supervised clinical training. A named licensed clinician has not been represented as having reviewed this atlas. Before public health-information promotion, the publisher should arrange subject-matter review and corrections where needed.
