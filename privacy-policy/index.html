How Corpus Anatomy Atlas works in your browser

Corpus Anatomy Atlas is an open educational website that requires no user registration, login account, or personal profile. Interactive anatomical diagrams, instant search across our 74 study guides, and self-test quizzes run directly inside your web browser. Standard web hosting logs (such as IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, and requested page URL) are processed by our hosting provider solely to deliver pages reliably and protect site security.

Cookies & Google AdSense advertising disclosure

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick DART cookie and AdSense cookies) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google processes data in advertising products, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by applicable law (including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), consent choices are presented via a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP).

External educational references

Our study guides include further-reading links to trusted public educational resources such as OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology, NCBI Bookshelf, and MedlinePlus. When you follow an external link, that destination website is governed by its own privacy policy and cookie practices.
