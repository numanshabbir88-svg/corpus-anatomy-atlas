How Corpus Anatomy Atlas works in your browser

Corpus Anatomy Atlas is an open educational website that requires no user registration, login account, or personal profile. Interactive anatomical diagrams, instant search across our 74 study guides, and self-test quizzes run directly inside your web browser. Standard web hosting logs (such as IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, and requested page URL) are processed by our hosting provider to deliver pages reliably and protect site security.

Atlas Q&A, optional AI answers, and external search

The assistant searches Atlas material first, including a server-side module of 5,000 Atlas-grounded Q&A records that is not copied into static website pages. A question answered by this local Atlas knowledge base is processed by the site's function and is not sent to an external search provider. External web search is currently not enabled because no provider credential is configured; an out-of-set question is not used to scrape Google or arbitrary websites, and the assistant may say it cannot verify an answer. If an optional server-side AI/search provider is enabled in the future, the question and selected response language may be sent to that provider under its terms and retention rules. For non-English requests, the assistant may also send the question or answer text to a translation service for translation under that service's own terms. Please do not submit names, identifying details, or sensitive personal/medical information. Provider credentials, if configured, are kept on the server and are not included in public JavaScript. The site does not intentionally write the question or generated answer to its function audit log; when an external provider is used, the server may record an audit ID, timestamp, model name, and public source titles/URLs returned by the provider. Hosting and security logs may separately include network information such as an IP address. External source names and links are not displayed in assistant answers; internal Atlas links are shown when relevant. The assistant uses reviewed Atlas images only and does not create new anatomy illustrations for answers. AI-generated answers can be incomplete or wrong and are for general education, not diagnosis or treatment.

Cookies & Google AdSense advertising disclosure

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick DART cookie and AdSense cookies) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google processes data in advertising products, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by applicable law (including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), consent choices are presented via a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP).

External educational references

Our study guides include further-reading links to trusted public educational resources such as OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology, NCBI Bookshelf, and MedlinePlus. When you follow an external link, that destination website is governed by its own privacy policy and cookie practices.
