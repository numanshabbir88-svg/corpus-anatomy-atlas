Overview

How this part of the body works

A hatched blastocyst attaches to receptive endometrium around the end of the first post-fertilization week, with timing variable. Its trophoblast differentiates into cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast, which interact with maternal tissue and begin early placental development; the inner cell mass gives rise to embryo proper. hCG supports the corpus luteum and progesterone early in pregnancy. Implantation within the uterine lining is distinct from normal fertilization in the uterine tube. The generated microanatomy is a conceptual section, not a patient biopsy or a claim about exact days. Ectopic implantation requires clinical assessment.

Questions people ask

Five questions about Implantation · blastocyst in endometrium

Short answers grounded in the structures described above. Use the illustrated guide for anatomical context.

Where does a blastocyst normally implant?

It typically attaches to receptive uterine endometrium, often in the upper or posterior uterus. Implantation within a uterine tube is ectopic and requires medical assessment.

When does implantation begin after fertilization?

Attachment commonly begins around the end of the first post-fertilization week, with variation. This is not the same as obstetric weeks counted from the last menstrual period.

What is the difference between cytotrophoblast and syncytiotrophoblast?

Cytotrophoblast is a cellular proliferative component; the outer syncytiotrophoblast is multinucleated and invades the endometrium. Both are early trophoblastic components, not a mature placenta by themselves.

What does early pregnancy hCG support?

Trophoblast-derived hCG maintains the ovarian corpus luteum so it continues progesterone production early in pregnancy. Later placental hormone production develops as the pregnancy progresses.

Are uterine implantation and tubal fertilization the same event?

No. Gamete fusion normally happens in a uterine tube. The conceptus then develops through divisions and reaches the uterus before a hatched blastocyst attaches to endometrium.
