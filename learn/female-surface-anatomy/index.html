Overview

How this part of the body works

Surface anatomy bridges what we see on the living body with what lies beneath, linking palpable skeletal landmarks to thoracic, abdominal, and pelvic organs. Use this page as an orientation map: click any glowing organ to jump into its full entry with the realistic render, labelled schematic and quiz. Within the human body, the female body — anterior (Corpus femininum) is situated in whole body — anterior view, receives arterial perfusion via systemic + pulmonary circuits, and is innervated by central + peripheral nervous systems.

The brain (Encephalon) control centre housed in the skull, glowing through the translucent scalp. Physiologically, the brain integrates sensation, movement, thought, memory and autonomic drive. Structurally, the thyroid gland (Glandula thyroidea) comprises butterfly-shaped gland on the front of the neck. Physiologically, the thyroid gland governs sets metabolic rate via T3/T4 under TSH control. The lungs (Pulmones) are paired thoracic organs flanking the heart. Physiologically, the lungs governs gas exchange across ~300 million alveoli. The heart (Cor) is muscular pump in the mediastinum, a little left of midline. Physiologically, the heart drives about 5 L of blood per minute through two circuits. Structurally, the liver (Hepar) comprises largest internal organ, right upper quadrant. Physiologically, the liver governs metabolism, detoxification, bile, plasma proteins. Structurally, the stomach (Gaster) comprises j-shaped reservoir under the diaphragm, left side. Physiologically, the stomach governs acid–pepsin digestion; intrinsic factor for B12.

Clinically, regarding the brain, stroke risk rises with age — remember FAST: face, arms, speech, time. From a diagnostic standpoint, in the thyroid gland, hypothyroidism causes fatigue, weight gain and cold intolerance. In clinical pathology involving the lungs, smoking destroys alveolar walls → emphysema. Medical evaluation of the heart also notes that coronary blockage causes myocardial infarction.

Questions people ask

Five questions about Female body — anterior

Short answers grounded in the structures described above. Use the illustrated guide for anatomical context.

Where are the main organs in the female body?

The brain is in the skull; heart and lungs are in the chest; digestive and urinary organs are in the abdomen; and the uterus, uterine tubes, ovaries and vagina are in the pelvis. The breasts lie on the anterior chest wall.

Which female structures need a deeper pelvic or breast view?

Ovaries, uterine tubes, cervix, pelvic supports and breast-tissue layers require dedicated regional chapters beyond the whole-body orientation view.

Where are the ovaries located on the female body map?

The ovaries lie on either side of the uterus in the lateral pelvis; the uterine tubes extend from the uterus toward them.

Where is the uterus located on the female body map?

The uterus lies in the midline of the pelvis, posterior to the bladder and anterior to the rectum.

Where is breast tissue located on the female body map?

The breasts lie on the anterior chest wall over the pectoral fascia; mammary tissue is within them, with ducts converging toward the nipple.
