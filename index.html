CorpusAnatomy Atlas

An open educational reference for human anatomy. Each chapter pairs a realistic digital medical render (thin label lines for visible structures) with a realistic-image focus mode and selected microscopic close-ups and unpinned deep-anatomy study notes. Each guide has further-reading links. The renders are digital educational illustrations, not patient images or substitutes for histology slides. Not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or an authoritative atlas.
