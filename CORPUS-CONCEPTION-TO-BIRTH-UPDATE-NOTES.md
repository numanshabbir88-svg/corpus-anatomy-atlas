# Corpus · conception-to-birth release · 19 October 2026

## Added

**11 independent study chapters**, in a navigable pathway: (1) sperm cell, (2) egg/secondary oocyte, (3) fertilization, (4) cleavage and blastocyst, (5) implantation, (6) placenta, (7) embryo, (8) fetus and pregnancy trimesters, (9) labor stage one—cervical dilation, (10) labor stage two—birth of baby, (11) labor stage three—delivery of placenta. Sperm and oocyte are separate branches BEFORE fertilization. The three labor stages have different endpoints.

Each chapter has 20 substantive anatomy or developmental entries, a three-question interactive quiz, five individually editable, visible, sourced educational SEO answers, and links to every step in the pathway. Existing reproductive chapters have incoming connections. Totals for the whole atlas: **74 chapters; 1,586 documented entries; 207 interactive quiz questions; 370 visible static Q&As; 79 HTML pages; 78 production sitemap URLs**. Older tooth, skull, foundations, muscle, skeleton and body-map chapters and interactions are retained.

This release now includes an **expanded picture-completion pass**: 17 original digital anatomical illustration plates across the project (seven from the first reproduction release plus ten new fetal, labor and microscopic-scale plates), and five original vector-style cleavage stage diagrams (one, two, four and eight cells and a compact 16-cell morula). Stage-specific first-, second- and third-stage labor cutaways and illustrative middle-/later-pregnancy plates replace the old nonpregnant primary chapter photos. Original sperm, egg, fertilization, blastocyst, implantation, attached placenta and undated embryo models remain.

The 11 chapters have **29 labeled gallery cards**; every one of their **220 entries** opens a relevant large digital teaching plate with a written picture-context note. Five tissue-level cutaways (seminiferous tubule, ovarian follicle, cortical reaction, embryonic neural folds and chorionic villus) add explicit two-scale comparisons. A few unmistakable micro structures have justified pointers; the only whole-cell visible markers remain three sperm structures and three oocyte structures. Microscope slides, patient ultrasound and real birth photos have **not** been fabricated. The fetal images cannot date pregnancy, the second-stage picture depicts descent BEFORE completed birth, and the third-stage picture depicts PARTIAL placental detachment rather than completed delivery. A separately unique true depiction of each of the 220 structures/events is NOT claimed. Entries that only have stage or regional context say so visibly.
