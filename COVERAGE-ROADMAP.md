### Conception-to-birth imagery disclosure — updated picture pass

The picture library now includes **22 original digital reproduction illustrations**, comprising 17 digital anatomical art plates and five original cleavage-stage diagrams (one cell, two, four, eight and compact 16-cell morula). Ten of the anatomical art plates were added in this pass: two illustrative fetal periods, three distinct labor stages, and five dedicated microscopic-scale testicular, ovarian, fertilization/cortical, neurulation and placental-villus models. Seven digital plates from the prior batch remain. **29 captioned picture cards** link these plates and three existing regional context examples across 11 chapters; all 220 entries have an enlarged relevant reference plate and truthful caption. Five microanatomy comparisons supplement the entry thumbnails. Sperm and oocyte retain the only six visible whole-cell markers; a few explicitly inspectable tissue structures receive micro-plate points, never a false pin on a gross-organ image. Fetus and labor still cannot be dated, measured or diagnosed from art; the second-stage view models descent, not a completed birth, and stage three depicts partial placental separation, not delivery. They are not microscope photos, actual obstetric scans or patient records. A unique real image for all 220 distinct structures/events does **not** exist; entries clearly say when a plate is only contextual. Clinical and specialist visual review remains pending.
