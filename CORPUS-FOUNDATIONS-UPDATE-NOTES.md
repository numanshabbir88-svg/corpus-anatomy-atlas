# Corpus anatomy atlas — foundation batch 1

## Included, not a claim that the entire requested syllabus is finished
- Three dedicated interactive chapters and crawlable English guides: `#/anatomicallanguage` / `/learn/anatomicallanguage/` (24 entries); `#/bodyregions` / `/learn/bodyregions/` (28 entries); `#/tissuefoundations` / `/learn/tissuefoundations/` (28 entries). All have three self-test questions and five visible, chapter-specific static Q&As.
- Nine inspected new digital teaching images: body planes, thoracoabdominal cavity cutaway, simplified generic cell, simple columnar epithelium, loose areolar connective tissue, skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle, smooth muscle and a multipolar neuron. Original PNG plus compact WebP/full and WebP/thumb copies are shipped. **None is a patient scan, real microscope slide or diagnostic study.** Tissue cutaways depict particular examples, not entire tissue families.
- Four-quadrant and nine-region patient-oriented charts are clickable in the atlas and crawlable anchors in the guide. Planes, deep cavities and surface maps do not receive invented anatomical pointers. The epithelial sheet and basal-lamina area have two approximate visible-feature pointers; remaining microscopic topics open relevant images without false pins.
- Existing skull, dental, 206 named skeleton bones, 45 joint families, 312 muscle records, gynecology, male/female images, body map, tissue lenses, search, animated focus, mini-image modals, on-page SEO and About contact remain. New chapters are prominently accessible from homepage and study-guide hub.
- Updated counts: **63 chapters, 1,366 chapter entries, 174 quizzes, 315 visible static Q&As, 68 HTML pages, 67 production sitemap URLs.** Anatomy foundations is a 14th browsing category, not a 14th organ system.
